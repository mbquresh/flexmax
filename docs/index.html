04 / Before you start

A few practical things.

FlexMax is in internal testing. Here’s what’s available today and what’s planned.

Can I download FlexMax now?

FlexMax is currently in internal TestFlight testing on iPhone. Public downloads and checkout aren’t available yet. You can email to request early access; a request does not guarantee an invitation or a release date.

What does it help with on day one?

Build a schedule, record what happened, and recover missed blocks around the time you have left. Pattern-based insights need enough recorded history; they aren’t promised on a fixed day.

Is there a desktop or Android app?

The current testing program is for iPhone. Desktop access is planned, but isn’t part of the available offer today. No Android launch date has been announced.

What will it cost?

Planned pricing is $14.99 monthly or $119 billed yearly (about $9.92 per month). Paid access is not available during internal testing. Pricing and trial details will be confirmed before launch. Requesting early access does not subscribe you or authorize a charge.

What happens to my reflections?

Your schedule and records are stored using Supabase. To generate an insight, recent completion information and your reflection text are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API. Read the privacy policy for storage, analytics, and deletion details.

What makes the insights different?

The app calculates the numbers before the AI writes an explanation. It checks recurring relationships and gives the model rules to avoid unsupported claims. Findings can still be incomplete or wrong; you can disagree with them in the app. Explore the engine.
