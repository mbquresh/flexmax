Reasonable questions.

I've quit five of these. Why is this different?

The ones you quit were mirrors. They showed your plan back to you, which is pleasant when you're doing well and brutal when you're not. None of them could tell you why a block keeps dying, because none of them were reading across your days. That's the entire thing this does.

Why is there no free trial?

Fair question, and here's the honest version: the rebuilding half works the hour you install it, so you're not paying to wait. The pattern-finding half does need about a week of real days before it says anything worth reading. As for why there's no trial at all — a trial recruits people who are browsing, and this doesn't work for people who are browsing. Paying first is the first thing you do that proves you've decided, and it's why the founding price is roughly half what it will be.

What happens in a week where I do almost nothing?

You come back, account for it, and the streak you're protecting stays intact — because it counts days you were honest about, not days you were perfect. Then you get a readable explanation of what happened instead of a wall of red. This is the specific moment the app was designed around.

What if it's wrong about me?

It will be sometimes, especially early. Every insight carries the counts it's based on, so you can check the reasoning instead of taking its word. The numbers stay small and honest on purpose — "four of your last five", never a dressed-up percentage.

Is my data used to train anything?

No. Your history is yours, and it's the thing that makes the app useful to you specifically. No ads, nothing sold.

Who is this genuinely not for?

Anyone who needs talking into wanting the thing in the first place — this assumes the wanting is already there. And anyone whose current system works. If you're getting where you want to go, there's nothing here you need.
